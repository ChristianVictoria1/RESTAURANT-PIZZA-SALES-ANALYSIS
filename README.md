
## Restaurant Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

-This project analyzes restaurant pizza sales data to identify revenue trends, customer ordering patterns, and opportunities to improve business performance.

-Using SQL and Power BI, I explored product performance, sales by category, order volume, and monthly revenue to develop data-driven recommendations for restaurant management.

## Business Objective 

-Identify which products and categories generate the most revenue, understand sales trends, and recommend strategies to increase revenue and support long-term profitability.

## Tools & Technologies

-MySQL: Data exploration, SQL queries, aggregations, and sales analysis.

-Microsoft Power BI: Data modeling, KPI cards, interactive visualizations, and dashboard development.

## Business Recommendations

-To boost revenue and support long-term profitability, the restaurant should focus on promoting high-performing pizzas, optimizing its menu, and improving inventory planning.

-Using the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard, management can identify the pizzas that generate the most revenue, compare category performance, and track monthly sales trends.

1. Promote Top Revenue-Generating Pizzas: Feature the top-performing pizzas in promotional campaigns and on the menu to maintain customer interest and encourage repeat purchases.

2. Optimize the Menu: Compare pizza sales volume and revenue by item and category to identify popular products and opportunities to improve underperforming items. Consider ingredient costs and profit margins before making menu changes.

3. Introduce Bundles and Upselling: Test meal deals and complementary add-ons to encourage customers to purchase more per order while monitoring whether the offers remain profitable.

4. Improve Inventory Planning: Use monthly sales trends and product demand to forecast ingredient needs, reduce food waste, and maintain sufficient stock of popular pizzas.

5. Monitor Profitability: Combine sales data with ingredient, labor, and operating costs to determine which products deliver the highest profit margins. Use these findings to guide pricing, promotions, and menu decisions.

## Expected Business Impact

-These strategies could help increase sales, improve inventory efficiency, and support more profitable business decisions.

-Their actual impact should be measured by tracking revenue, average order value, food waste, and profit margins over time.

## Key Takeaways

-This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to turn raw transactional data into a business-focused sales analysis.

-The dashboard provides an overview of revenue, order volume, menu performance, category trends, and monthly sales.

-The analysis establishes a foundation for more informed decisions regarding menu optimization, inventory planning, promotions, and profitability.
